03 · Approach

My program development lifecycle.

Design

Define who the program serves, what success looks like and the shape of the learner journey.

Build

Templates, trackers, SOPs, LMS cohorts and communication flows before anything goes live.

Launch

Onboard mentors and participants, brief facilitators and check readiness.

Manage

Run live delivery, moderate communities and flag risks early.

Measure

Track attendance, completion, feedback and outcomes, then report plainly.

Iterate

Use the data to fix the next cycle and document what was learned.
